Uncut

Record a YouTube stream from its first minute, keep your own unedited copy, and read it back with a summary. It works while the stream is still live.

How it works
  1. Record. yt-dlp --live-from-start rewinds to the stream's first minute and keeps up with the live edge until it ends or you stop it. Only the audio is saved, about 1 MB a minute.
  2. Transcribe. Whisper runs on this Mac's GPU. When a stream ends it transcribes on its own, and later updates only add the new audio.
  3. Summarize. OpenAI writes a short summary with timestamped key points, and your phone gets a ping when it's done.
  4. Keep it. Times in the margin play your own copy, including any ending they cut from the published version. Save to Obsidian puts the summary and transcript in your vault.
